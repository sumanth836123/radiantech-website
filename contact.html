COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Radiantech Systems Pvt Ltd on custom hardware design, IP ownership, and production lead times.

What is the typical turnaround time for a custom PCB prototype? ↓
For standard multi-layer carrier board prototypes, our turn-around time is typically 2 to 3 weeks from schematic approval to assembled functional prototypes. Rapid 10-day expedited prototyping is available for urgent projects.
Who retains Intellectual Property (IP) rights for custom designs? ↓
You retain 100% full ownership of all custom schematics, PCB Gerber files, FPGA bitstreams, and software source code developed under a custom engineering contract. We sign non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) before discussing technical details.
What are your Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ) for production? ↓
We support low-volume production runs starting at just 10 units for specialized industrial & defense deployments, as well as full volume manufacturing for thousands of units.
Do you offer CE / FCC certification and compliance support? ↓
Yes. We design all hardware with EMC/EMI pre-compliance standards (CISPR 32 / FCC Part 15 Class A & B). We assist throughout accredited lab testing to secure CE, FCC, RoHS, and REACH certifications.
