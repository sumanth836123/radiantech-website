Layer 4: Intelligent Edge Applications INTELLIGENT SOFTWARE

The software users see and act on: video analytics applications, real-time alerts, operator dashboards and reports. Systems integrate with existing control rooms, video management and enterprise platforms through standard APIs and messaging, and are managed remotely with secure over-the-air updates.

Analytics Applications Alerts & Dashboards System Integration Secure OTA Updates
Layer 3: Edge AI & Vision Engine EDGE AI

The intelligence of the system. We select, train and optimise AI models to run in real time on embedded hardware, building accelerated multi-camera video pipelines and fusing data from vision, thermal, radar and RF sensors to detect, classify and track reliably in the field.

Model Training & Optimisation Accelerated Inference Multi-Camera Pipelines Sensor Fusion
Layer 2: Embedded Firmware & System Software EMBEDDED FIRMWARE

The software that brings the hardware to life: board support packages, customised embedded Linux, device drivers and bootloaders, real-time firmware for microcontrollers, and FPGA logic for high-speed signal processing, all built with secure boot and long-term maintainability in mind.

BSP & Embedded Linux Device Drivers RTOS Firmware FPGA & DSP

The physical foundation: edge computing platforms built on NVIDIA® Jetson Orin™, custom carrier boards and multilayer PCB design, camera, sensor and RF interfaces, PoE and wireless connectivity, and rugged, fanless enclosures engineered for heat, dust and vibration.

NVIDIA® Jetson Orin™ Custom Boards & PCB Sensor & RF Interfaces Rugged Fanless Design
